        About the directory and data

        Academic highlights are the largest reported undergraduate program areas by award share (PCIP), not a measure of fame or teaching quality. The field filter uses reported bachelor’s programs and award areas. Graduate-only schools may not have comparable data.

        Size means undergraduate degree/certificate-seeking enrollment. Tuition includes annual tuition and required fees before financial aid. Living expenses combine reported room/board and other expenses, preferring on-campus figures; they exclude books and are not a personal budget estimate. Out-of-state tuition is not a guaranteed international student price.

        International community means the reported nonresident undergraduate percentage. Citation counts are OpenAlex totals matched by institution name or unique website and location; coverage is partial and counts favor larger research institutions. Employment and average earnings describe limited, historical federally aided cohorts, not all alumni.

        Employment rate is calculated as working-and-not-enrolled ÷ (working-and-not-enrolled + not-working-and-not-enrolled), 10 years after entry. It includes non-completers and excludes people still enrolled; it is not a job-placement rate or an unemployment statistic. The published cohort map dates these counts to 2020–2021. Average annual earnings use the discontinued mean earnings measure for working, non-enrolled federally aided former students 10 years after entry: measured in 2014–2015, adjusted to 2017 dollars. Earnings include wages and positive self-employment income. Reporting periods differ; do not treat these as current outcomes. Missing or suppressed data remains “Not reported.”

        Missing values are shown as “Not reported.” With an active numeric filter they are excluded unless you choose to include them. Images are from university websites or Wikimedia. Campus images are preferred; a university logo or seal is clearly labeled when used instead. Missing images are labeled, and source/license credits are linked.

        Sources: College Scorecard · Metric definitions · OpenAlex. School data is bundled for offline searching; images and source links need internet access.
